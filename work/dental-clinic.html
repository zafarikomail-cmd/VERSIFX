The Business

A fictional practice, built to prove a real point.

"Arcstone Dental" is a fictional dental practice created by VERSIFX to demonstrate our thinking, design approach, and technical capabilities. It is not a real business, and this is not a completed client engagement.

We chose a dental practice because the shape of the problem is familiar to almost any local, appointment-based business: a website that needs to build trust quickly, explain services clearly, and turn a visitor into a booked appointment — on both desktop and mobile.

The Challenge

The problems a practice like this typically faces.

An outdated website that undersells the quality of the practice.

A poor mobile experience, despite most visitors arriving on a phone.

Unclear services, leaving new patients unsure what's actually offered.

A weak appointment journey with too many steps between interest and booking.

A difficult contact process — buried phone numbers, no clear next step.

A lack of clear trust signals for someone comparing several practices.

The Opportunity

Every one of those problems is friction.

A prospective patient comparing a handful of practices rarely calls to ask questions — they judge on what they can see in thirty seconds. Slow-loading pages, unclear services, and a booking process that asks too much too soon are enough to send them to the next search result.

Closing that gap doesn't require reinventing the business. It requires a digital presence that removes friction at every step — from the first search to the confirmed appointment.

Mobile Experience

Most patients arrive on a phone. It should show.

The mobile layout isn't a shrunk-down desktop site — it's re-ordered around what a phone visitor actually needs first: what the practice offers, and how to book it.

  • — One thumb-reachable booking action, always in view.
  • — Shorter copy, larger type, generous tap targets.
  • — A hero image that leads, not a navigation menu that competes for space.
