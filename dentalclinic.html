The Business

A fictional practice, built to prove a real point.

ArcStone Dental does not exist. It's a fictional dental practice created solely to demonstrate how VERSIFX approaches a real, common business problem — a local, appointment-based practice whose digital presence isn't working as hard as the business behind it.

We chose a dental practice because its challenges are typical of many appointment-based local businesses: patients need to trust a provider quickly, understand services at a glance, and book without friction. Everything shown here is conceptual design and development work, not a completed client project.

The Challenge

What a typical practice is up against.

Common, realistic problems we designed this concept around — the kind of friction that quietly costs a local practice new patients.

An outdated website that doesn't reflect the quality of care patients actually receive.

A poor mobile experience, even though most prospective patients arrive from a phone.

Unclear services — visitors can't quickly tell what's offered or whether it fits their need.

A weak appointment journey that asks too much, too soon, before trust has been built.

A difficult contact process, with hours, location, and phone details scattered or hard to find.

A lack of clear trust signals — nothing that quickly reassures a new patient they're in good hands.

The Opportunity

Every point of friction is a patient who chooses someone else.

None of these problems are dramatic on their own. But together, they add small moments of doubt and delay to a decision patients want to make quickly. A visitor who can't tell what's offered, can't book easily on a phone, or can't find a phone number will often just move on to the next search result — usually a competitor with a smoother experience, not necessarily better care.

The VERSIFX Approach

How we'd think through this project.

01
Discover

Understand how patients currently search for, evaluate, and choose a dental practice — and where the existing site loses them along the way.

02
Diagnose

Pinpoint exactly where the experience breaks down — mobile usability, unclear services, or a booking flow that asks too much too soon.

03
Design

Build the new experience around one clear goal: make booking an appointment the obvious next step within seconds of arriving.

04
Build

Turn the design into a real, responsive website with a streamlined services section and a genuinely simple booking flow.

05
Grow

Define how the practice would track visits and bookings after launch, so the site keeps improving instead of sitting still.

The Solution

A clearer site, built around trust and booking.

Three core surfaces of the concept: a services section that removes guesswork, an about section that builds trust fast, and a booking experience that respects a patient's time.
