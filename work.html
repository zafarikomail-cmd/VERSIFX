Healthcare

Dental Clinic Digital Transformation

Problem

An outdated website, a poor mobile experience, a weak booking journey, and weak online visibility were keeping the clinic from being found and chosen.

Approach

Discover the patient journey end to end, diagnose where prospective patients drop off, then design around a single, obvious next step: booking an appointment.

Solution

A new website, an improved booking flow, a mobile-first experience, and a clear customer journey from first visit to appointment.

Restaurant Digital Experience

Problem

A strong in-person experience wasn't matched online — inconsistent hours, no clear reservation path, and a menu that was hard to find.

Approach

Treat the website as the front door: fast, mobile-first, and built around the two things guests actually want — the menu and a table.

Solution

A digital-first ordering and reservation journey, a refreshed presence, and a consistent voice across web and social.
